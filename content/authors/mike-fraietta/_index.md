After receiving a degree in physics in 1979 I started my career at
the Goddard Institute for Space Studies (GISS) in New York City.
At GISS I worked with the scientists there to investigate the nature
of the particles suspended in the Jovian atmosphere using Pioneer
and Voyager data. After moving to Houston in 1982 I began a long
career in aerospace. In 1984 I received an M.S. in Physical Science
from the University of Houston at Clear Lake City. I was involved
with many exciting programs including: Space Shuttle, Space Station,
Starliner, Iridium and the NEAR mission to the asteroid Eros. I
retired in 2022 and moved to North Adams (to be closer to family)
and now work as a part-time guidance consultant for the Starliner
project.
