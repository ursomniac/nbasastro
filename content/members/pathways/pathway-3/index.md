# Pathway 3 — No Setup

You write your article in whatever format suits you, collect your files
into a directory, ZIP it, and email it in. No installation required.
The site administrator handles the conversion and formatting.

**You will need:**
- Nothing — just a way to write and a way to send email

---

## Write Your Article

Write your article content in any format you are comfortable with:

- A Word document (`.docx`)
- A Google Doc (export as `.docx` or `.pdf`)
- A plain text file (`.txt`)
- An Apple Pages document

Write as you normally would — use headings, paragraphs, and lists.
Do not worry about Markdown or front matter — the administrator will
handle that.

If you know what DSOs, planets, or other objects your article references,
include a note listing them so the administrator can add the correct
metadata.

---

## Gather Your Media

Collect all images you want included in the article.

- Place them in the same folder as your article document
- Use descriptive filenames so it is clear which image is which
- Note in your document where each image should appear
- See [Media Guidelines](/members/media/guidelines/) for size limits —
  resizing helps but is not required for this pathway

---

## Create Your Directory

Put your article document and all images into a single folder.
Name the folder after your article topic, e.g. `m42-observation` or
`january-meeting-recap`.

---

## ZIP and Submit

See [Submit by ZIP](/members/submission/submit-zip/) for instructions on
creating a ZIP file and emailing it to the site administrator.

Include a brief note with:

- What the article is about
- Any objects (DSOs, planets, stars) it references
- Any series it belongs to
- Any preferred publication date

---

## After Submission

See [After Submission](/members/submission/after-submission/) for the
review process and timeline.

The administrator will contact you if any information is missing or if
changes are needed before the article goes live.
