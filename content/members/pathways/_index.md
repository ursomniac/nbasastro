# Contribution Pathways

There are four ways to contribute an article to the NBAS site.
Choose the one that fits your setup and comfort level.

---

## Which Pathway is Right for Me?

**Do you have git and Hugo installed locally?**

Yes → [Pathway 1 — Full Git](/members/pathways/pathway-1/)
You can write, preview, and submit entirely from your local machine via pull request.

**Do you have Hugo installed but want a simpler submission process?**

Yes → [Pathway 2 — Local Preview](/members/pathways/pathway-2/)
Write and preview locally, then submit your article as a ZIP file by email.

**Do you just want to write without any local setup?**

Yes → [Pathway 3 — No Setup](/members/pathways/pathway-3/)
Write your article in any format, ZIP the directory, and email it in.
The administrator handles the rest.

**Do you prefer a browser-based editor with no files or ZIP involved?**

→ [Pathway 4 — CMS](/members/pathways/pathway-4/)
Coming soon. A web-based editor that commits directly to the repository.
