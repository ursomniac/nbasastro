# Where Articles "Live"

For the most part in the site structure they're under `/content/articles/YYYY/MM`
(where YYYY is the year and MM is the 2-digit month).  

Under _that_ directory, each Article has it's own directory.

Scenario: you're writing an Article about a star party you attended.  You've picked out
5-6 images you want to show.

Let's say it's August 2026.

So under `content/articles/2026/08` you create a _new_ directory: `cool-star-party`.
(There's no "rule" on these directory names, but try to avoid spaces in the name.)

In that new directory there's only _one_ required file:   `index.md`  - that's 
your article file. (Yes, it has to be called `index.md`)

You put your images in the same directory alongside `index.md`.   That's it.

All the **work** is what you **do** in `index.md`.
