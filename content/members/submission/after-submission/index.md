# After Submission

Here is what happens after you submit an article, whether by pull request
or ZIP file.

---

## Review

All submitted articles are reviewed by the site administrator before
going live. The review covers:

- Front matter is complete and correctly formatted
- Images are present, correctly named, and appropriately sized
- Content renders correctly in Hugo
- No styling overrides or raw HTML in the article body

---

## Turnaround Time

Typical turnaround is **a few days or less**. If you have not heard back
within a week, feel free to follow up.

---

## Feedback

If changes are needed, the administrator will contact you with specific
notes. For ZIP submissions this will be by email. For pull requests,
feedback will appear as comments on the PR on GitHub.

---

## Going Live

Once approved:

- **ZIP submissions** — the administrator places your article directory,
  confirms it builds correctly, and merges it to the live site.
- **Pull requests** — the administrator merges your branch to `main`.
  The site rebuilds automatically on GitHub.

You will be notified when your article is live.

---

## Contact

For questions about a submission in progress, contact the site administrator
at **[submissions@nbasastro.org]**.
