Every month the sky offers something worth going outside for. This series is your guide to what's up right now — the best objects for the season, the events worth staying up for, and the context that makes what you're seeing more than just a light in the sky.

Each installment works whether you're at a telescope, holding binoculars, or just standing in the driveway.
