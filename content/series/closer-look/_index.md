Every object in the sky has a story that goes beyond its coordinates.

This series takes one object at a time — a planet, a nebula, a galaxy, a cluster — and looks at it whole: what it is physically, how it was discovered, what we've learned about it since, and what you'll actually see when you point your scope at it. The science and the experience together.
