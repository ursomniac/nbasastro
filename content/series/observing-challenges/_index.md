Once you're past the basics, the sky starts asking questions. Can I see that? Can I reach that with what I have? This series is where those questions become challenges — organized by skill level and equipment so you can find ones that are actually within reach, and maybe a few that will surprise you.

Some of these you'll stumble across naturally. Others nobody would think to attempt until someone points out that it's possible. That's half the fun.

<h3>Skill levels:</h3>
<ul>
<li> <b>Beginner</b> - things to try to get you used to look at the sky, and noticing what's up there!
<li> <b>Intermediate</b> - you know a few things: these will help you learn new concepts,
and hone your skills!
<li> <b>Advanced</b> - you've got some experience, but you want to extend and test your skills!
</ul>
<h3>Observing Modes:</h3>
<ul>
<li> <b>Naked Eye</b> - you just need YOU (and maybe a star map and a red flashlight)
<li> <b>Binoculars</b> - scanning the skies with binoculars (a tripod helps)
is a great way to discover objects
<li> <b>Telescope</b> - if you have a telescope - these challenges will get you comfortable with using it
<li> <b>Imaging Telescopes</b> - for "smart" scope owners - now you get to see what these
scopes are capable of!
</ul>
