Organized by how you observe and your skill level.

Skill levels:

  • Beginner - things to try to get you used to look at the sky, and noticing what's up there!
  • Intermediate - you know a few things: these will help you learn new concepts, and hone your skills!
  • Advanced - you've got some experience, but you want to extend and test your skills!

Observing Modes:

  • Naked Eye - you just need YOU (and maybe a star map and a red flashlight)
  • Binoculars - scanning the skies with binoculars (a tripod helps) is a great way to discover objects
  • Telescope - if you have a telescope - these challenges will get you comfortable with using it
  • Imaging Telescopes - for "smart" scope owners - now you get to see what these scopes are capable of!
