--- title: "Observing Challenges!" --- Some challenges to undertake! Organized by how you observe: - Naked-Eye Challenges: what objects are "above your nose" in the skies above you! - Binocular Challenges: discovering some of the brighter DSOs/SSOs! - Telescope Challenges: finding interesting objects with your telescopes! - Imaging Challenges: things to discover with your smart scope or astrophotography rig!
