---
title: "Gareth Talks about Asteroids"
description: "Dr. Gareth Williams discusses asteroid names, orbits, and origins."
---
Thousands of asteroids get named every year. Most names pass without notice — but some are surprising, some are poetic, and some raise an eyebrow. Dr. Gareth Williams, NBAS member and longtime researcher at the Minor Planet Center, picks out the ones worth a second look.
