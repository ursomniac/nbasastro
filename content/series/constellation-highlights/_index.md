The constellations are humanity's oldest map. Every culture that ever looked up drew pictures in the stars — and many of those stories, some thousands of years old, are still the names we use today.

But a constellation is more than its mythology. Each one is a region of sky with its own cast of objects worth finding: bright stars, double stars, clusters, nebulae, galaxies. This series covers both — the stories and the sky — with a guide to what's actually there and how to find it, whatever equipment you're using.
