Smart scopes are a great tool for exploring the cosmos. This series is your field guide to understanding what you find.

The practical articles are here when you need them — which scope, how to use it, how to push it beyond its defaults. But the deeper purpose of this series is something most astronomy coverage misses entirely: your images are data, and data tells stories.

Every object you image has a history written into it. Nebulae show complex structures shaped by the birth and death of stars. Galaxies carry the scars of ancient interactions. Star clusters capture stellar evolution frozen in a single frame. The universe left clues everywhere — and you collected them.
