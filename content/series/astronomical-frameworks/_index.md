---
title: "Astronomical Frameworks" 
description: "The classification systems, measurement tools, and defining concepts that astronomers use to make sense of the universe."
---
Astronomy is built on frameworks — the tools and definitions astronomers use to classify, measure, and make sense of the universe. The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram. The spectral sequence. The Hubble morphological types. The magnitude scale. This series examines those frameworks: where they came from, what they reveal, and why they matter to anyone who looks up.
