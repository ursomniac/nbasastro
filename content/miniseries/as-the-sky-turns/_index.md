title: "As the Sky Turns"
description: "One motion, Earth spinning about the celestial pole, explains the nightly turn of the stars, which ones never set, why each season has its own constellations, rotating smart-scope frames, and the changing tilt of the ecliptic and the Milky Way. Then the pole itself moves."
---

What motions are responsible for the changing patterns on the sky?  This miniseries takes them one at a time, to explain how constellations move over the course of a night and across seasons, why some stars never set (or rise), how the Sun's position on the ecliptic shifts, and how to tell when an object is "up".
