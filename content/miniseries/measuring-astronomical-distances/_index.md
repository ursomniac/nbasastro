---
title: "Measuring Astronomical Distances"
description: "From the Earth-Sun distance to the edge of the observable universe: what astronomers' distance units mean, and how each one was actually measured."
---
How far away is the Sun? A star? Another galaxy? This ten-part miniseries works outward one scale at a time. At each scale, one article builds a feel for the unit astronomers use, and another tells the story of how that distance was actually measured.

Each part stands on its own, but they read best in order.
