Astronomy has no shortage of compelling stories to tell — and NBAS members are happy to
tell them. Since 2023 we've given over a dozen public talks covering topics from
naked-eye observing to deep sky objects, the solar system, stellar evolution, and more.

## Talks at Our Meetings

Some of our monthly meetings are structured as public presentations — held at the
North Adams Public Library on the first Wednesday of the month at 6 PM, open to anyone.
These are a great low-key way to hear about a topic in astronomy and meet the club.
Watch our calendar and newsletter for which meetings include a public talk.

## Talks at Observing Events

When a public observing night is sponsored by a local institution, we'll often pair it
with a short introductory talk before heading outside — giving attendees some context
for what they're about to see through the eyepiece.
