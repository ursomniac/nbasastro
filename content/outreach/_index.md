The Northern Berkshire Astronomical Society believes that astronomy belongs to everyone.
Since our founding in 2023, we've delivered over a dozen public talks and hosted public
observing events across the region — bringing telescopes, expertise, and enthusiasm to
libraries, community gatherings, and schools.

If your organization is looking for a speaker, a public observing session, a star party,
or a classroom visit, we'd love to hear from you.

## What We Do

- **Public Talks** — presentations on a wide range of astronomy topics, open to all
- **Public Observing** — telescopes out, skies open, everyone welcome
- **Star Parties** — dedicated dark-sky evenings for groups and organizations
- **School & Classroom Visits** — bringing astronomy to students at all grade levels

## Our Track Record

Since 2023, NBAS has delivered **12+ public talks** at libraries, community centers,
and civic organizations, and hosted **public observing events** at dark-sky sites
across the Northern Berkshires. All events are free to attend.
