Several times each season, NBAS members set up telescopes and invite the public to come
out and look up. These events are free, open to all ages, and require no experience —
just curiosity and a willingness to stay up past sunset.

## Public Observing Nights

We host regular observing nights at accessible public locations in the Northern Berkshires —
typically open fields, parks, or nature areas with good southern horizons and minimal light
pollution. Dates are announced on our calendar and through our newsletter.

Some events are organized entirely by NBAS; others are hosted by local institutions such
as museums or nature centers, which may include an introductory talk before heading outside.
Either way, our members bring the equipment and the enthusiasm.

What you might see on any given night depends on the season — planets, the Moon, star
clusters, nebulae, and galaxies are all fair game. We'll point to whatever is up and
looking its best.

## Private & Group Sessions

Public nights not your thing, or need something tailored to your group? We can arrange
observing sessions for schools, scout troops, civic organizations, camps, and other groups —
scheduled at a time and location that works for you.

[Contact us](/contact/) to discuss what you have in mind.
