1. Our Commitment

We believe the internet should be available to everyone. We are committed 
to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, 
regardless of ability or technology. 

2. Current Status

Our website is built using the Hugo static site generator. By using static HTML 
and avoiding complex dynamic scripts, we aim to ensure our content is inherently 
compatible with modern web browsers and assistive technologies like screen readers. 

3. Efforts to Support Accessibility

While we have not undergone a formal accessibility audit, we strive to 
maintain a high standard by:

* Using Semantic HTML to provide clear document structure.
* Ensuring Keyboard Navigation is possible through standard browser behavior.
* Maintaining a minimalist design to ensure fast load times and clear readability. 

4. Limitations

As a small, independently managed site, we are aware of some potential limitations:

* Older images may lack descriptive "alt text."
* External links to third-party sites may not meet the same accessibility 
standards as our own pages. 


5. Feedback and Assistance

We welcome your feedback. If you encounter any barriers or have difficulty 
accessing any part of this site, please reach out.
