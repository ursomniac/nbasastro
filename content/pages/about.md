# The Northern Berkshire Astronomical Society

* Founded 2023

## Mission Statement

**Purpose:**

The purpose of the Northern Berkshire Astronomical Society is to foster the hobby of astronomy by bringing together individuals interested in the study of the universe.

**Goals:**

_Observation_: To promote, educate, and facilitate the appreciation of astronomy

_Outreach_: To educate the general public and school community about the science of astronomy.

_Community_: To provide a welcoming environment for hobbyists of all levels to share knowledge and experience.

_Resources_: To facilitate the use of telescopes, astrophotography equipment, and study materials.

## Services and Outreach

The club offers several ways to interact:

* Monthly Meetings: Meets monthly at the North Adams Public Library on the first Wednesday of every month, 6 PM

* Public Observing: Several events are held through the year at locations around the Berkshires

* Public Talks: Several members are available to give presentations on a wide range of topics in astronomy.

## Membership

Membership is open to all persons (18+) who support the Club’s purpose and have 
a bona fide interest in astronomy.
