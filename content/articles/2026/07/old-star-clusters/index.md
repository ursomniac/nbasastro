Notes:

* Berkeley 17: Oldest known open cluster; sits at a low galactic latitude near the plane.
* NGC 6791: Extremely metal-rich (over twice the Sun's iron content), defying standard age-metallicity rules.
* NGC 188 (Caldwell 1): Also known as the Polarissima Cluster; highly circumpolar for Northern Hemisphere viewers.
* Berkeley 32: Survives by orbiting out in the less dense, outer regions of the galactic disk.
* King 2: A faint, highly condensed cluster positioned far in the outer galaxy.
* NGC 2243: One of the most metal-poor open clusters known, located far below the galactic plane.
* M 67: Closest ancient cluster; chemically near-identical to our Sun, making it a critical stellar laboratory.
