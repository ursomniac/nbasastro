## Introduction

I was able to attend a one-day workshop on astronomy accessibility 
for the visually impaired at the Springfield Science Museum, led by
Noreen Grice author of the book "Touch the Stars"

This is an intriguing and amazing topic:  how do you explain concepts in astronomy like the Moon, or solar flares, or Saturn's rungs, or galaxies using other
senses than sight?

## Local Immersion

The Springfield Science Museum as a commitment to accessible science, and has 
several exhibits that are tactile - for example you can experience the Moon 
by touching reliefs with craters matching the surface.   Other materials
likewise let you explore Saturn's rings, comets, and solar activity.
