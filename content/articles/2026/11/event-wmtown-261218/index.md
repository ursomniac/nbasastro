## Dec 18: Williamstown Public Lands (Sheep Hill), Williamstown MA - 6:00 PM

{{< nbas-image src="banner.webp" fullwidth="true" >}}

Join NBAS at a public event coordinated with [Williamstown Rural Lands](https://rurallands.org/) on **Saturday, November 28**
for an evening under the stars at their Sheep Hill property: a laser-pointer tour of the autumn sky, followed by a look through telescopes. No experience or equipment needed, and families are welcome.

## Location and Directions

{{< nbas-image src="Sheep_Hill_farmhouse_over_pond_LRE.webp"
caption="Sheep Hill at Williamstown Rural Lands" >}}

**Williamstown Public Lands**
- When: December 18, (TIME)
- Address: 671 Cold Spring Rd, Williamstown, MA 01267
- Phone: (413) 458-2494
- Website: [https://rurallands.org/](https://rurallands.org/)

From Field Park (the roundabout) in Williamstown, drive south on Route 7.
After 1.4 miles, Sheep Hill is on the right.

All NBAS Public Events are free to the public.   Please review our [Event Rules](/outreach/event-rules/) page.

## What to Know Before You Go

- **Accessibility:** this is an outdoor event - be prepared for walking on uneven ground.
- **Bundle Up!** it can get cold quickly especially waiting to look through the telescopes.
