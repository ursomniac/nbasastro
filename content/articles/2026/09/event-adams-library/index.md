## Oct. 8: Public Lecture at the Adams Free Library, Adams MA - 6:30 PM

How many planets are there?  How should Pluto (and the other "dwarf" planets) fit in?  With over 6,000 confirmed exoplanets in almost 5,000 systems, how should we think about "planets" as we go deeper and learn more about solar systems?

{{< nbas-image src="talk_banner.webp" fullwidth="true" >}}

NBAS member Dr. Bob Donahue will present a public talk on this topic at two events in October.
