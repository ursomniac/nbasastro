Springfield Museums, the STARS Club, Arunah Hill, and the Astro Society invite you to be a part of “Space for Everyone!” This virtual symposium on October 30 will focus on encouraging, supporting, building, and expanding capacity for astronomy enjoyment through outreach and informal learning. 

This will be an exciting opportunity for participants to learn, network, and discover new ideas and information about effective methods to bring astronomy to the public.  “Space for Everyone,” will feature a lineup of engaging presenters, including keynote speaker Astronaut Cady Coleman.

Recurring themes include emerging science that builds excitement about space, tools for enhancing outreach and education, and best practices in astronomy outreach and education. Information will be presented in multiple formats, including lecture, discussion, and demonstrations.  

Registration Fees includes live, virtual attendance to the October 30, 2026 Virtual Symposium, event recordings through the registration portal until 1/31/27.
