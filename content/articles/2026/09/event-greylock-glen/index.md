# Oct. 10: Public Lecture *and* NBAS Observing Event at Greylock Glen, Adams MA - 7:00 PM

How many planets are there?  How should Pluto (and the other "dwarf" planets) fit in?  With over 6,000 confirmed exoplanets in almost 5,000 systems, how should we think about "planets" as we go deeper and learn more about solar systems?

{{< nbas-image src="talk_banner.webp" fullwidth="true" >}}

NBAS member Dr. Bob Donahue will present a public talk on this topic at two events in October.

After the talk, we'll gather to view the skies with telescopes 
brought in by the NBAS.   

(Yes, Saturn will be on the observing list!  Maybe Neptune too!)

## Location and Directions

Greylock Glen Center
**165 Gould Rd., Adams MA, 01220**

[Get directions on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UG9Gy4oKYjTUUSCL9)

Parking is in front of the center. 

## What to Know Before You Go

- **Weather / cloud policy:** the talk happens rain or shine; telescope viewing afterward is weather-dependent. Any changes will be posted on this website — check back before you head out if conditions look uncertain.
- **What to bring:** even early October evenings can turn chilly once the sun's down, so dress warm. A red flashlight, if you have one, helps preserve everyone's night vision once we're observing.
- **Cost:** free. 
- **Accessibility:** both the center and parking lot are accessible.
- **Event Rules:** please review our [Event Rules](/outreach/event-rules/) before attending.
