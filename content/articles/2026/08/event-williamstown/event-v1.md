# Sep. 12: Public Observing Event at Williamstown Historical Museum

{{< nbas-image src="banner.jpg" fullwidth="true" >}}

We're excited to announce an NBAS Public Observing Event on September 12th at the Williamstown Historical Museum.

{{< nbas-image src="talk_banner.png" fullwidth="true" >}}

Things start off at 7PM with a public lecture: "30 Years of Exoplanets: What have we learned" by NBAS member,
Dr. Bob Donahue showing how the discovery of exoplanets has completely changed our understanding of how the solar
system formed, what types of planets are common around other stars, and the continued search for Earth-like planets.

After the talk, we'll gather to view the skies with telescopes brought in by the NBAS.
