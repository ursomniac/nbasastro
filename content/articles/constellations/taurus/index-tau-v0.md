## The Bull

(NOTE: painting is likely an aurochs, there are spots around the face, a semi-circle around the eye with other spots on either side of the figure that look suspiciously like the Pleiades and the Belt of Orion.)

Taurus is a very old constellation - so much so that depections of it may even show up in a cave painting at Lascaux,
from over 10,000 years ago.
