### Why seven? (HYG v4.1, within 1.5° of center — computed)
- Six bright: Alcyone 2.85, Atlas 3.62, Electra 3.72, Maia 3.87, Merope 4.14, Taygeta 4.30.
- 0.75-mag gap → Pleione 5.05 (variable) → HD 23753 5.44, Celaeno 5.45, 18 Tau 5.66, Asterope 5.76.
- → limit ~5 gives six; a darker sky adds several at once, not one.
- Pleione is 5.0′ from Atlas (computed), 1.4 mag fainter.
- Keep the observed facts separate from any claim about where "missing sister" myths came from.
### Pleione = the variable-star answer
- GCVS Gamma Cas type, 4.77–5.50 V. Phases: B / Be / Be-shell as its disk forms and dissipates;
  shell 1938–54, again from 1972; Be 1989–2005; back to Be-shell 2005–2019 (Marr et al. 2022,
  arXiv 2202.05900, model: cycle repeats every 34 yr). Binary, 218-day orbit (Nemravová 2010).

### Distance dispute
- Hipparcos 1997: 118.3 ± 3.5 pc; van Leeuwen 2009: 120.2 ± 1.9. Others 132–135 pc.
- Stakes: low metallicity or unknown young-star physics if Hipparcos right.
- HST FGS 2005 (Soderblom): 3 stars, 133.5 pc. VLBI 2014 (Melis): 136.2 ± 1.2. Gaia DR2: ~136.
- Cause never pinned down; van Leeuwen not convinced in 2014. Say "Hipparcos was wrong here",
  not why. Link: building-the-ladder (MS fitting).
### Age / brown dwarfs
- LDB: lithium burns at ~3 × 10^6 K; at 125 Myr the boundary falls at 0.075 ± 0.005 M☉ → every
  member fainter than it is a brown dwarf.
- Dinosaurs: cluster formed while they lived. 125 Myr ≈ Yixian Formation feathered dinosaurs
  (2021 dates ~125.8–124.1 Myr); 78 Myr = Late Cretaceous. Period boundaries: verify at
  https://stratigraphy.org/chart.
### Members
- Lodieu et al. 2019: 1,248 Gaia members within tidal radius; stream to 40 pc from center.
- Gaia DR2 ML study: 1,454 (p > 0.6), D = 136.0 pc; four high-mass members being ejected.
