## Hook
"Returns" = back in the evening sky: by mid-January it's up by early evening and climbing
every week toward opposition. This apparition has a bonus the last few didn't: we're looking
edge-on to the moons' orbits, so the moons eclipse and occult each other — something you only
get about every 6 years (twice per 12-year orbit).
