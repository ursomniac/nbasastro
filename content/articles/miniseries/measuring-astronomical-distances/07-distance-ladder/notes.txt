## Fact pass 1 + 2 (2026-10-29) — settled
- Parallaxes c.1900: ~60 stars (CSIRO ATNF); ~100 with accuracy <50% (Mignard, NED); 1890: 40,
  1910: 300 (Arenou/Mignard table). Russell 1910 claims s.e. ~40 mas → beyond ~25 pc parallax
  ≈ error (computed).
- Herschel: star gauges did NOT require equal brightness (Timberlake, arXiv 1112.3635: uniform
  spacing + telescope reaching the edge); he used the equal-brightness idea elsewhere. Text uses
  only the Sirius unit (verified in A06). Common textbook error — avoid in later chapters.
- Secular parallax: Sun ~20 km/s vs disk stars ≈ 4 AU/yr (Popowski & Gould, astro-ph/9703140;
  4.09 AU/yr vs LSR, Hopkins glossary). Kapteyn used it (van der Kruit, arXiv 1407.2632).
- Moving cluster: Boss 1908 (AJ 26, 31), 41 proper motions + 3 radial velocities. van Altena 1974
  (PASP 86, 217): convergent lines cross in 15°×10° patch; one subset → 27 pc; Hyades = zero point
  of Galactic scale and (via cluster Cepheids) extragalactic. Perryman 1998: 46.34 ± 0.27 pc.
  Gaia DR2: 47.5 pc (ϖ 21.052 mas; Gaia Collab. 2018, A&A 616 A10).
- MS fitting: Johnson 1957 ZAMS calibration; Hyades distance he used 15% short → ~0.3 mag errors
  (van Leeuwen 2009, arXiv 0902.1039).
- NGC 2158: 4.69 ± 0.22 kpc (Gaia-era study, arXiv 2012.06342) ≈ 5.5× M35. CONSISTENCY:
  galactic-anticenter says "about 4×" (APOD 2021-05-10) → add to consistency.yaml.
- Pleiades: see figure values. Cause: errors correlated over Hipparcos 0.9° field (van Leeuwen &
  Evans 1998; Makarov re-reduction) — An et al. 2007 (astro-ph/0607549).
- Spectroscopic parallax: Adams & Kohlschütter 1914; Kohlschütter noticed line-ratio discrepancies
  correlated with proper motion (arXiv 1511.05930); ~20% accuracy claimed (Nature 1921).
  Mechanism: giant's low-pressure atmosphere → narrower lines, relatively stronger ionized lines
  (OpenStax-based textbook, UCF Pressbooks).
- Leavitt: 1908 Annals HCO 60, 1,777 variables, brighter → longer periods (16 stars); 1912 HCO
  Circular 173, 25 SMC variables, signed Pickering, first sentence credits Leavitt; hoped for
  parallaxes of some variables.
- Hertzsprung 1913: statistical parallax of 13 Cepheids; M ≈ −2.3 at P = 6.6 d (Baade 1956 PASP
  68, 5). SMC parallax 0.0001″ (Shapley 1918 ApJ 48, 154) ≈ 33,000 ly; printed 3,000 ly.
  Sandage/Tammann version differs — text uses only the consistent part.
- Cepheid physics: Eddington 1917/1926 valve; period–mean density; Zhevakin 1953 He II.
- RR Lyrae: HB, He core ~0.5 M☉ nearly independent of progenitor mass (arXiv 2208.04332);
  M_V ≈ +0.6; ~50 L☉; RR Lyr V 7.06–8.12, P 0.567 d, 270 ± 20 pc. Evening: 29° alt, az 303 at
  6:30 PM EST Dec 30; 20° Jan 15.
- Baade's Window: Baade 1946 RR Lyrae; 1951 R0 = 8.7 kpc (arXiv 1107.3152).
- Baade 1952: Rome IAU, Sept 1952; no RR Lyrae in M31 at V ≈ 22.2 with the 200-inch; ~1.5 mag;
  Thackeray SMC RR Lyrae; Shapley 1953 AAS → credit dispute. 1.5 mag = ×2.0 distance.
- Trumpler 1930 (Lick Obs. Bull. 420): ~100 open clusters; ~0.7 mag/kpc.
- δ Cep: Goodricke 19 Oct 1784; η Aql (Pigott) 10 Sep 1784; P 5.366 d; V 3.48–4.37; Benedict 2002
  HST π = 3.66 ± 0.15 mas (astro-ph/0206214) → 273 pc; Anderson 2015 244 ± 10 pc; L ≈ 1,900 L☉
  (Gallenne 2021, via Wikipedia). AAVSO comparisons: ζ Cep 3.6, ε Cep 4.2. Evening alt 60° at
  6:30 PM EST Dec 30; 52° Jan 15; 44° Jan 31; az ~315.
- Polaris: P 3.969 d; Hipparcos 133 pc; Turner 2013 99 pc; Bond 2018 HST (Polaris B) 160 pc;
  Gaia DR2 Polaris B ~137 pc.
