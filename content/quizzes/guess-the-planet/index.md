        - text: "Venus"
          image: "venus.jpg"
          correct: false
          explanation: "Venus rotates slowly - so slowly that if you were standing on Venus (and there were no clouds), the Sun would rise in the West and set in the East, but there are no \"double\" sunrises."
        - text: "Pluto"
          image: "pluto.jpg"
          correct: false
          explanation: "Pluto has a longer rotation than most planets or dwarf planets, but it moves around the Sun so slowly that the effect of a \"double\" sunrise could never happen."
