## Format (from the template: last month's "Solar System Object Features", 2026-11-17)
- Front matter: title, date, series ["astronomy-quiz"], level, description, banner, thumbnail.
- `quiz.renderer: "text-choice"`, `passing_score: 70`.
- 12 questions; each has `id`, `image`, `text`, and four `answers` with `text`, `correct`,
  `explanation`. Every option gets a real explanation that teaches something.
- Correct answer position rotated across questions, recorded in a comment above each question
  ("# Q1 — topic — correct at position 3"). Aim for an even spread (each position 3×).
- Body: `{{< quiz >}}` only.
- Template style worth copying: wrong answers are near-misses that point to the right object
  elsewhere ("Right planet, wrong moon").
